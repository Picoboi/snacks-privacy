Snacks & Sorcery: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Snacks & Sorcery is a single-player game. This policy explains what happens with your data when you play it. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use the internet at all.

What is stored on your device

Your saved game (your party, progress and items) and your settings (vibration, screen shake, battle speed) are stored only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them, and so does "Start over" in the game's Settings.

Permissions

The only permission the app uses is vibration, for short haptic feedback during fights (you can turn it off in Settings). It does not request internet access.

Purchases

This version of the game has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added in a later version, payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the game never sees your payment details. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
